FAQ

Questions

What is Lockbox?

Lockbox is an iPhone vault app that encrypts photos, videos, IDs, cards and passwords on your phone. It opens only with a pattern you choose, and each pattern can open its own vault. There's no server and no account, so neither we nor Apple can see inside.

What if I forget my pattern?

Open the vault with the 12-word recovery phrase you received when you created it. If you lose both, no one can recover it.

Can Apple or Lockbox see my files?

No. Files are locked on your iPhone before they're saved. In iPhone and iCloud backups they stay locked, and the key never leaves this iPhone. The encrypted backup you create opens only with your recovery phrase.

Do I need to sign up?

No. No account, email or phone number.

What happens when I switch phones?

Create an encrypted backup in Settings and save it to iCloud Drive or Files. Restore it on the new phone with the backup file and your recovery phrase.

Which is better, 3×3 or 5×5?

With 25 dots, a 5×5 pattern is much harder to guess. For quick access, use a pattern together with Face ID.

How many vaults can I make?

3 for free, unlimited with Pro. Draw a new pattern on the lock screen to create a vault that opens with it. Give each vault a name and color so you always know which one is open.

Can I use it in English?

Yes. Lockbox follows your phone's language, and you can switch between English and Korean in Settings.
